RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is made on January 1, 2026, by and between Horizon Property Management LLC ("Landlord"), and Jordan Taylor ("Tenant").

1. Premises and Term
Landlord leases to Tenant the residential property located at 742 Evergreen Terrace, Suite 3B ("Premises"). The lease term shall be twelve (12) months, commencing on February 1, 2026, and ending on January 31, 2027.

2. Rent and Security Deposit
Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,400 due on the first (1st) day of each calendar month. A late fee of 15% of the total monthly rent shall automatically apply to payments received after the third (3rd) day of the month. Tenant shall deposit $4,800 as a security deposit upon execution. Landlord may retain the full security deposit if Tenant terminates early or fails to provide sixty (60) days advance notice of non-renewal.

3. Maintenance and Repairs
Tenant shall maintain the Premises in good condition and shall be solely responsible for all maintenance and repair costs exceeding $50 per occurrence, including structural, plumbing, and HVAC systems, regardless of cause. Landlord shall have no obligation to make structural or major system repairs during the lease term.

4. Right of Entry
Landlord or its agents may enter the Premises at any time, with or without prior notice, for inspection, maintenance, showing to prospective tenants or buyers, or any other business purpose.

5. Subletting and Guests
Tenant shall not assign this lease, sublet any portion of the Premises, or permit guests to stay longer than three (3) consecutive days without Landlord's express written consent. Unauthorized guests shall incur a penalty of $100 per day.

6. Utilities and Services
Tenant shall be responsible for ordering, maintaining, and paying for all utility services, including water, gas, electricity, trash removal, and internet. Landlord shall not be liable for utility interruptions.

7. Indemnification and Liability
Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold Landlord harmless from any and all injuries, damages, claims, liabilities, or legal fees occurring on the Premises, including incidents caused by Landlord's failure to maintain common areas. Tenant must maintain tenant insurance with a minimum of $500,000 general liability coverage naming Landlord as additional insured.

8. Termination and Holdover
Should Tenant remain in possession of the Premises after expiration of the term without Landlord's written consent, Tenant shall pay holdover rent equal to 300% of the standard monthly rate, computed daily.

9. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
